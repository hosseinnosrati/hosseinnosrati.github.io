Academic & Engineering Biography

I am an Electrical Engineering researcher specializing in Autonomous Electronic Design Automation (EDA), Biomedical Digital Signal Processing (DSP), and Resource-Constrained Edge TinyML. My work focuses on bridging theoretical signal analysis with deterministic, zero-malloc ANSI C99 firmware execution on bare-metal silicon, as well as connecting large language models (LLMs) to closed-loop physical circuit simulation and KiCad manufacturing.

I am the first author of a published research preprint with an official DOI (10.13140/RG.2.2.16561.75366), evaluating zero-phase IIR arrhythmia telemetry against the gold-standard MIT-BIH PhysioNet database with 100% sensitivity. Additionally, I authored two flagship open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers (circuit-design-anything and hardware-debug-anything) enabling AI agents to deterministically synthesize analog circuits and debug ARM Cortex-M silicon registers.
