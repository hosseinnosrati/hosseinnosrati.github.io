About Me

I am an Electrical and Telecommunications Engineering researcher specializing in Digital Signal Processing (DSP), TinyML, and Resource-Constrained Embedded Systems. My research bridges statistical signal processing theory with deterministic, zero-allocation ANSI C99 firmware execution on bare-metal architectures (such as ARM Cortex-M and low-power IoT microcontrollers).

My core technical focus centers on formulating energy-efficient telemetry pipelines for healthcare wearables and wireless cyber-physical sensing. This includes non-distorting zero-phase IIR/FIR filter chains, real-time Pan-Tompkins QRS morphological tracking, closed-loop adaptive acoustic filtering (NLMS) for hearing assistive devices, non-contact cardiopulmonary RF sensing via Wi-Fi CSI phase sanitization, and sub-Nyquist Compressive Sensing (CS) with Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (OMP) sparse recovery.

Low-Latency Deterministic Arrhythmia Telemetry via Zero-Phase IIR Filtering and Static Decision Forests on Resource-Constrained Edge Microcontrollers

An ultra-low-latency (0.12 ms/beat) DSP and TinyML edge telemetry pipeline combining forward-backward zero-phase IIR conditioning, Pan-Tompkins QRS morphological detection, Welch LF/HF spectral HRV decomposition, and a 16.3 KB zero-malloc ANSI C99 static decision forest.

Featured Research & Engineering Projects

Edge-Hearing-Aid-DSP: Clinical Acoustic Architecture & Adaptive Feedback Cancellation

A real-time embedded audio DSP engine operating under strict medical latency budgets (<10 ms). Features a 64-tap adaptive Normalized Least Mean Squares (NLMS) filter for howling feedback cancellation, subband spectral noise subtraction, 6-band clinical audiogram fitting (NAL-R prescription), and Wide Dynamic Range Compression (WDRC).

Benchmark: 0.805 ms frame latency (10× faster than 8ms budget) • +4.2 dB SNR gain • 4.0 KB C99 Header
View Repository → ANSI C99 Header Streamlit App

Contactless-Vital-RF-Sensing: Wireless Respiration & Heartbeat Telemetry

Non-invasive physiological monitoring extracting sub-millimeter chest wall displacements from 5.8 GHz RF phase shifts and Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI). Incorporates carrier frequency offset (CFO) regression, phase unwrapping, and dual-band IIR filtering separating thoracic respiration (0.1–0.5 Hz) from cardiac apex vibrations (0.8–2.0 Hz).

Benchmark: 0.001 ms/packet latency (>9,400× real-time speedup) • <0.4 breaths/min & <0.5 bpm estimation error
View Repository → Streamlit App

IoT-Compressive-Sensing: Sub-Nyquist Biomedical Telemetry for Wearable Nodes

Asymmetric compressive sensing pipeline designed to reduce on-air wireless transmission volume by 50% to 70% in IoMT battery-operated nodes. Employs random sub-Gaussian projections on-node and gateway Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (OMP) sparse recovery in the DCT basis with CRC-16 telemetry framing.

Benchmark: 0.005 ms/block node encoding • 0.24% PRD clinical error (<2% AAMI limit) • 52.3 dB SNR
